- Got a chance to stop by another dollar tree and this one had adam readi board. It's almost twice as light as my other foam board! I bought a bunch of it, and constructed new wings + horizontal/vertical stabilizers. I was inspired by a video that used blank plastic cards to add structural support, and i hot glued them to the bottoms of the wing + horizontal stabilizer and it works really well as a way to attach the carbon fiber fuselage to the wing. Although now that I've done it, I think a 3d printed attachment, possibly with hot glue, tape and/or rubber bands might make for something better. Overall very exciting, my airframe is 80% of the way there. I'll maybe try to add some weight to the front and test fly tomorrow as a basic glider. After that, it'll be time for the power system. Currently the whole aircraft is about 120g which is perfect. The lightness of the adam's readi is allowed me to make a folded-over wing that has a much better air foil shape. We'll see if it makes a big difference but either way, I'm improving my construction.
