- Really interesting ideas for 3d printing a glider + making a basic glider with a propeller: [link](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QsPQ2E3cZaA). The big issue with 3d printed aircraft parts is the weight. Couple takeaways:
	- carbon fiber rod for sturdiness in fuselage
	- 3d printing a frame on tissue paper
	- moving the wings upward to avoid prop wash
	- natural dihedral was interesting
	- curious what software he used to simulate the structural integrity
	- heat gun to add curve to plastic frame
- I should try to recreate this, but for the rubber band motor.
- Also interesting to note flight time as a metric. I've been going for basic flight/sort of vague undefined aesthetics of flight. I should make this more concrete that maybe I should consider flight time as something to shoot for with my designs.
