- I lost the screw on the tip of the motor to seal the propeller. Tried an m5 nylon bolt, worked great.
- Hot glued another piece of foam board to the bottom of the fuselage. This will form an I-beam structure so it does bend as much, which should help stabilize the direction of the propeller.
- Printed new extrusion connectors so I can widen the launcher. This way I can have more room for the tail to pass through. Before I noticed if I stiffened the tail with another rubber band, it turns out it was wacking the launcher on the way out the whole time.
- I looked up some ideas for counteracting the force from the propeller. The first thing was to just tilt the propeller the opposite direction. This actually seemed to work alright. I got a launch where the plane mostly went straight and a little down, but it still veered right a little bit. And it's still pitching up slightly.
	- Thinking about how to fix this. Pitching up is pretty consistent. I think I can move the propeller on top of the fuselage to fix this.
	- For twisting right, I might try and tape a quarter to the left wing or something. That should help roll the plane left, but if the twisting mainly yaws then this might be tougher. Let's see..
