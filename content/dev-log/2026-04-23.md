- My glider with the propeller flew fine. For this scale of plane, i don't think the size of my propeller and the strength of the rubber bands was enough to generate meaningful thrust. Common with these scrappy homemade projects, the propeller holster got all beat up, and my rubber bands broke pretty easily after winding them up too much. However, I'm still happy with this generally, the design did the basic function of what I was imagining, and that's enough for now. For this initial glider project, I just wanted to make something that demonstrated the rough concepts of an aircraft, even if they didn't show ideal results. My next project(s) will be more exciting. I'm going to actually set some goals for what the aircraft should do and try to achieve them by any means possible. To be continued.
