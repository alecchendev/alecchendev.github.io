	- Wingspan: 140cm - roughly 2x foam board
	- Wing chord of 18cm - aspect ratio of roughly 8
	- Wing taper - going to disregard this for now
	- Horizontal stabilizer - roughly 30% of wing area -> 140\*18\*0.3 = 756 cm^2 - going to go with 50cm span 15cm chord
	- Vertical stabilizer - say half the horizontal stabilizer -> 25cm vertical, 15cm chord
	- Tail moment arm - distance from wing aerodynamic center to tail aerodynamic center (roughly 25% from leading to trailing edges) - ratio of this to wing chord should be 2.5-4 -> 4\*18cm = 72cm
	- Fuselage length - length to wing span is usually 0.6-0.8 -> 84-112cm
