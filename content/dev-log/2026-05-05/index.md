- I'm pretty happy with this launcher overall. It launched 130g glider a farther distance than the 90g from before. The glider in the video kinda nose-dived, but that makes sense since we're launching it pitched up to start, whereas gliders are optimally thrown more like a dart, straight ahead. This launcher is really just meant to get something in the air with some momentum, and I think for my goal of 100g, this will be perfectly fine. And realistically this is intended for my future aircrafts that have propulsion systems, so this should be enough.
- There's some things that'd be nice to clean up but overall I think this is in a good place, and I can move on to other things.
- Things i'd clean up:
	- Add weight, especially in the front. Maybe add some traction on the bottoms the leg mounts. I noticed the launcher shift and lifts up slightly.
	- Make the m3 holes in the sled and launch mount slightly larger, they tend to be pretty tight.
	- Add more of a hook shape to the v-mount hooks for the springs so they stay on easier even on dry shots.
	- Use the highest energy springs in my kit, or buy better springs. The more power the better for the most part on this launcher.
