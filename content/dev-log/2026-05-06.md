- What's next? Revisiting my goals, I started making this launcher so I could launch an aircraft consistently, and ideally get 10 seconds air time. Launcher is good to go, now let's do the aircraft. I initially said I'd re-make a glider, but I'm going to skip that. I'm just excited to make something with propulsion, and this launcher was really meant for that kind of aircraft anyway. There are a lot of decisions to be made here. Where to start...
- First thing is I want to do electric propulsion. This is mostly because I'm interested, and it will start getting closer to something that will further apply to larger aircrafts.
- Requirements
	- Starts from launcher
	- Flies straight for at least 10 seconds
	- Uses electric propulsion of some sort
- Some broad ideas
	- general design - probably something pretty simple/standard, a basic plane with a propeller at the front. Fuselage, wings, horizontal + vertical stabilizer.
		- I should look into variations and decide what I want. For example, should I have a high or low wing, or both?
	- materials - i want something sturdy enough to survive crashes without me reconstructing the plane (i'm also curious about how to preserve my propeller in crashes), but something lightweight, and easy to iterate on. I saw tim stanton in [this video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XPXN0QejqM0) 3d printed a wing with "lightweight PLA" which I didn't really know was a thing.
	- propulsion - just going to put a propeller at the front with the motor. I think that should be simple and functional. Will need to design the propeller.
	- goal weight - my launcher can handle my test 130g glider okay. Based on feeling, <=100g feels like it'd be a decent goal, but this is flexible.
	- should calculate all the aerodynamic properties I want - brainstorming an approach...I feel like the motor + battery weight:power ratio may be my biggest constraint. I wonder if I should start there, and then design around a couple of my options there.
	- should consider doing some sort of wind tunnel tests or simulations, could be cool/fun.
