*Written during my time building Prometheus with Atelier Missor.*

- I don't feel this anxiety anymore.
- I'm uncertain about my future, but i'm okay with that.
- I've noticed I think a lot about how much i love my girlfriend
- I don't really like the bubble i've been in i don't think. i like the people i've been with lately
- i feel like i'm part of something when i'm wearing the uniform. i noticed this most when i changed into normal clothes at the airport. in the uniform, i feel like i'm somebody. in my normal clothes, i don't really.
- flight is amazing. one thing i hope for the future is that general aviation becomes prolific.
- i feel like i can more easily filter the signal from the noise. when i see things online, it's all these people trying to appear a certain way, but in real life i see the actual work, and it's clarifying what really matters.
- i used to feel every minute of sleep i missed. Lately ive been getting less sleep than normal, eating really late, etc. but im happy
- I feel really present on this adventure. At the end of the day i fall asleep in minutes. My mind isn't buzzing. It feels so good to drink water/gatorade after sweating and working in the heat. Being really thirsty and just downing gatorade like the world is ending. That and the violent afternoon breeze. Standing in the shade and throwing my arms out like i'm on the titanic. Nothing like it.
- Certain positions, with the sun, hot days, itchy polish paste, bronze dust all over my skin, it sucks. But then the breaks are so much better. I have weird skin rash spots that i tend to each night.
- My hands and parts of my face have been green since a couple days in. I got this special heavy duty soap that works better than normal soap, but ive never gotten it all out.
- The morning heat is the worst with no wind. Pretty quickly i feel my body raining sweat droplets. But at least i know its doing its job of cooling me down. The afternoon is hotter but there is wind. By the evening, it can be quite pleasant to work.
- The sky is so beautiful here. There are always beautiful clouds.
- It is nice to see the sunset everyday. I spot venus each night and see it move.
- It feels good just to stand up tall. With no heavy item to carry, no position required for working, no tent blocking overhead.
- Adventurous things happen constantly. Something about this group and being out here attracts adventure from the world.
- Lots of interaction with people when they recognize us. Few ppl think its odd, many people just think its cool, some people thank us for what we're doing.
- Nobody in the world is doing this.
- i get why people have trucks. there are a lot of heavy things to transport if you work on physical things. tools. argon bottles. generators. metal. concrete. pallets.
- lately i haven't been so worried about things, just appreciative of everything. life, the world, people, they're all so awesome.
- could i go back to sitting on a computer in an office all day? i don't know.
- i feel free. nobody was really holding me back before. but just between this adventure and just being unemployed in LA, i feel far more free now. freedom can't be hypothetical. it's only there if it's exercised. if you had the ability to do anything in the world, but you chose to spend your time in a prison cell, would you feel free?
